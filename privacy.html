Data we collect

  • Enquiry form: name, email, phone, destination, dates, message, whether you asked for flights only, and any marketing boxes you tick.

  • Contact form: name, phone and message.
  • Guide request and Umrah interest: the details you type into those forms.
  • Cookie choice, stored in your browser. On the holiday checklist page, the packing items you tick are also stored in your browser.
  • If you allow analytics: usage data through Google Analytics 4.
  • If you allow marketing, and an ID has been added: data collected by Meta and TikTok.
  • Prayer times: the city you select is sent by your browser to Aladhan at api.aladhan.com.
  • Weather on the holiday page: the destination in the link is sent by your browser to Open-Meteo.
