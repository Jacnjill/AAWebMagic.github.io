Privacy Notice

Version 2. Last updated: 28 November 2026

The short version

  • We're TripNTravel, an independent UK travel agency. We use your details to answer your enquiry, find you a holiday and book it for you.
  • We only ask for what we need to do that.
  • We share booking details with the holiday companies and airlines whose products we sell, so they can provide your trip.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not pass it to other companies for their own marketing.
  • We only send you holiday news if you've said yes. You can change your mind at any time.
  • We don't currently record calls. If we start, we'll tell you at the start of every call.
  • You pay through a secure payment link. We never take your card details by WhatsApp or phone.
  • You have rights over your information, including seeing it, correcting it and complaining to the ICO.

1. Who we are

TripNTravel (also written Trip N Travel) is the trading name of TRIPNTRAVEL PRIVATE LTD. We're an independent travel agency and a member of PTS. Our website is tripntravel.co.uk.

In data protection law we are the "controller" of your personal information. That simply means we decide how it's used, and we're responsible for looking after it.

We're a travel agent. We help you choose and book holidays and flights from the holiday companies and airlines whose products we sell. Those companies provide your trip.

2. How we get your information

Most of what we hold comes straight from you, when you get in touch by:

  • WhatsApp (we use the WhatsApp Business platform, provided by Meta)
  • Phone call
  • Email
  • A form on our website
  • A Facebook or Instagram lead form or advert (Meta passes us the details you enter)

If you book for other people, such as your partner or children, you give us their details too. Please make sure they know you're sharing them with us.

3. What information we collect

When you enquire, we may ask for:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Where you'd like to go, your travel dates and your departure airport
  • How many people are travelling, and the ages of any children
  • Board basis (for example, all inclusive or half board) and a rough budget
  • Your messages to us, and our notes from phone calls
  • Your marketing choices, and a record of when and how you made them (see section 5)

When you book, we also need:

  • Each traveller's name exactly as it appears on their passport
  • Dates of birth
  • Passport details, if the holiday company or airline needs them
  • Any special requirements you tell us about
  • A payment reference (never your full card number)

Health and accessibility needs. Special requirements can sometimes include health or accessibility information, such as a wheelchair request or a medical condition. The law treats this as "special category" data. We only collect and use it where we need it to arrange your travel, with your explicit consent or where the law otherwise allows.

Dietary and faith-related requests

You can ask for things such as halal meals, an alcohol-free hotel or prayer facilities. These requests can reveal religious belief, which the law treats as special category data. We use them only to arrange your holiday and pass them to the supplier only so they can arrange it. We note them as a request on your booking, not as a label about you. Our AI assistant may process them. We treat your request as your explicit consent for this purpose, and you can withdraw it at any time by telling us.

Children. We only collect children's names and ages as part of a family holiday enquiry or booking, and we get them from the adult who is booking.

Payments. You pay through a secure payment link produced on the PTS system, and your payment goes into the PTS merchant account. We do not take your card details ourselves, whether by WhatsApp or by phone.

Calls with our advisers

We don't currently record calls. If we start, we'll tell you at the start of every call.

4. Why we use your information (and our legal reasons)

The law says we must have a valid reason (a "lawful basis") for each use. Here's what we do and why:

  • Replying to your enquiry and preparing quotes: because you've asked us to, as a step before a possible booking (a contract).
  • Making and managing your booking: to carry out our contract with you.
  • Meeting our legal and accounting duties: because the law requires it (legal obligation).
  • Keeping records, preventing fraud, improving our service, training our team and handling complaints and disputes: our legitimate interests in running a good, safe business. We balance these against your rights.
  • Sending holiday news: only with your consent (see section 5).
  • Health or accessibility information: with your explicit consent, or where it's necessary to arrange your travel and the law allows it.

5. Holiday news and offers: your choice

We'd love to send you holiday news, but only if you want them. We ask separately for each channel (for example WhatsApp). You can say yes to one and no to the others.

Asking us about a holiday does not count as saying yes to marketing. We'll reply to your enquiry by WhatsApp, phone or email, including sending you quotes, but we won't send you marketing unless you've opted in.

On our website enquiry form, there is a tick box, not ticked for you, that says: "Yes, I'd like to receive holiday news from Trip N Travel by WhatsApp. I can unsubscribe at any time by replying STOP or by telling you." You can send the form without ticking it.

Our records. If you tick it, we record that you did, the date and time, the page of our website you used, and the version of the wording you saw. We keep this as proof of your choice.

You can withdraw your consent at any time, for any channel.

How to tell us to stop. Just reply STOP on WhatsApp (or "unsubscribe", "opt out" or "don't message me"), or tell us in any other way, by phone, email or message. We'll record your request and stop.

If you have a live booking, we may still need to send you essential messages about it, such as payments due, changes to your holiday or travel disruption. These are service messages, not marketing.

6. Who we share your information with

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not pass it to other companies for their own marketing.

Holiday companies and airlines. When you book, we give the holiday companies and airlines whose products we sell the details they need to make your booking. They use your information as separate controllers, under their own privacy notices.

PTS. Payment links and booking records are handled through PTS, which acts under its own terms and its own privacy notice. We can give you a link to it if you ask.

The service providers who help us run our business. They act on our instructions, apart from limited purposes of their own, such as security and keeping their services running, which they explain in their own privacy notices:

  • HubSpot: our customer records system (CRM). Your data is held in an EU data centre, but HubSpot is part of a US-owned group.
  • Meta: its WhatsApp Business platform, which we use to send and receive our WhatsApp messages, and Facebook and Instagram lead forms and adverts
  • 3CX and Twilio: 3CX is our phone system provider. Twilio carries our phone calls. We don't currently record calls.
  • Supabase and Netlify: hosting for our sales app and our website
  • Google (Workspace, Drive and email) and Microsoft (email): our office and email tools
  • Anthropic: the provider that helps write our WhatsApp replies and picks out your travel details so a search can be run. It processes your enquiry messages and trip details (such as your first name, destination, dates, party size, budget, the occasion and your message). When our advisers use AI tools, it can also see names, phone numbers and recent messages for that enquiry. It acts under our instructions. It is based in the US (see section 7). Our systems try to pass messages about health, accessibility, children, visas, complaints or refunds to a person instead of the AI, but they cannot catch everything. Please don't put health or other sensitive details in the chat. Ask for a team member instead.
  • xAI: It processes our phone calls, including transcription of what is said and the assistant's spoken replies, and runs holiday searches. For searches, it receives only your travel details (for example destination, airports, dates, number of nights, number of travellers and children's ages, room and board type, baggage, transfers and budget), not your name or phone number. It acts under our instructions. It may process data in the US (see section 7).

How we use AI to help with your enquiry

Trip N Travel (TRIPNTRAVEL PRIVATE LTD) uses an AI assistant to help us answer holiday enquiries quickly. This section explains what it does, what it keeps and the limits we have placed on it.

What the AI assistant does

  • It replies to WhatsApp messages you send us and helps answer phone calls to our enquiry line. You are always told you are talking to an AI assistant, and if you ask, it will never claim to be a person.
  • It uses the details you give us (for example where and when you want to travel, how many people are going and your budget) to search for holiday options for you.
  • It helps our team prepare quotes and follow-up messages.

Who processes your messages

  • WhatsApp messages are processed by Anthropic (the provider of the Claude AI model), acting on our instructions. This includes picking out your travel details so a search can be run.
  • Phone calls, including transcription of what is said and the assistant's spoken replies, are processed by xAI, acting on our instructions.
  • Holiday searches are run by xAI's AI service, acting on our instructions. It receives only your travel details (for example destination, airports, dates, number of nights, number of travellers and children's ages, room and board type, baggage, transfers and budget), not your name or phone number. It then checks prices on public holiday booking websites in the same way a member of the public would, without signing in. It stops before any guest details are entered and never books or pays, so your name and contact details are not given to those websites.
  • WhatsApp messages are delivered through Meta's WhatsApp Business service.

We only share what is needed to deal with your enquiry. These providers act under contract as our processors, may not use your details for their own purposes, and do not use your messages to train their AI models. Anthropic, xAI and Meta may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is protected by the standard contractual clauses approved for the UK together with the ICO's UK Addendum, which form part of our contracts with them.

Notes the AI keeps about you

To avoid asking you the same questions twice, the AI assistant keeps short notes for each customer: a summary of what you are looking for and a short list of next steps. These notes are deleted automatically 90 days after your last message to us. Records we must keep for a booking or for legal reasons are kept separately, as explained elsewhere in this notice.

A person checks before anything important happens

The AI assistant cannot make decisions about you on its own. A member of our team reviews and approves:

  • any message to you that includes a price
  • every quote
  • every follow-up message
  • every change to your customer record

The AI assistant cannot take payments or confirm bookings.

Marketing and opting out

We do not send you marketing messages unless you have agreed to receive them. Reply STOP at any time, or tell us you do not want to hear from us, and we will stop messaging you. This is always honoured, including by the AI assistant.

Why we can use your details this way

We use your details to deal with the enquiry you made and to take steps towards a booking you ask for (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), and for our legitimate interest in answering enquiries quickly and accurately (Article 6(1)(f)). Because a person approves every price, quote, follow-up and record change, no decision with legal or similarly significant effects on you is made solely by automated means.

Your choices

You can ask to speak to a person at any time. You can also ask us what notes we hold about you, ask us to correct them or ask us to delete them, by contacting info@tripntravel.co.uk. You have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

7. Sending information outside the UK

Some of our providers are based in the US or process data outside the UK. Where that happens, we rely on UK-approved safeguards, such as the UK-US data bridge or standard contractual clauses with the UK Addendum, and we only use providers that offer them. Please contact us if you would like more information. Two of these are Anthropic and xAI, the AI providers that help us write replies, handle phone calls and run holiday searches (see section 6). They may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. We rely on the standard contractual clauses approved for the UK together with the ICO's UK Addendum for these transfers, which form part of our contracts with them. Please contact us if you would like a copy.

8. How long we keep your information

We only keep your information for as long as we need it:

  • Enquiries that don't lead to a booking: 12 months
  • Booking and accounting records: 6 years, the usual UK standard
  • Records of your marketing choices: until you withdraw your consent, and after that we keep a note that you opted out, so that we don't contact you again
  • Messages in our WhatsApp and website chat, including those written by our AI assistant: we keep a full copy in a restricted log as evidence of what was said. Only a few authorised staff can see it. We keep it for as long as the enquiry or booking it belongs to, then delete it.

After that, we delete it or make it anonymous.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you (access)
  • Ask us to correct anything that's wrong or incomplete (correction)
  • Ask us to delete your information (erasure)
  • Ask us to limit how we use it (restriction)
  • Ask for your information in a format you can take elsewhere (portability)
  • Object to how we use it, including for marketing (objection)
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent

Some rights only apply in certain situations, and we may sometimes need to keep information for legal reasons. If so, we'll explain why.

To use any of these rights, contact us at info@tripntravel.co.uk, or message or call us in the usual way. We'll reply within one month. If your request is complex, we may need up to two more months. If so, we'll tell you why within the first month.

10. Cookies

Our website uses cookies. We explain these in our separate Cookie Notice.

11. Questions and complaints

If you have a question or you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please talk to us first at info@tripntravel.co.uk. Our office hours are 7 days a week, 9:00am to 8:00pm UK time. We'll do our best to put it right.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator: ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

12. Changes to this notice

We'll update this notice if the way we use your information changes, and we'll change the date and version at the top. If a change affects you in a significant way, we'll let you know.
