2. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your computer, phone or tablet when you visit it. They let the website remember things, such as your cookie choice, or count how many people visit a page.

Similar technologies work in much the same way. They include tags or pixels (small pieces of code on a web page that send information to another company when the page loads) and local storage in your browser. In this notice, "cookies" covers all of these.

The law (the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, known as PECR) says we must ask for your consent before we use anything that isn't strictly necessary for the website to work. Where cookies collect information about you, data protection law (UK GDPR) applies too.

3. The types of cookies we use

Strictly necessary (always on)

We save your cookie choice (Accept all or Reject all) in your browser's local storage, so we don't ask you again on every page. The website needs this to work properly, so we don't need your consent for it. Before you make a choice, we don't set any other cookies.

Analytics (off unless you click Accept all)

Analytics help us understand how people use our website: how many people visit, which pages they look at, and how they found us. We use this to improve the site. The analytics service we use is Google Analytics, provided by Google.

We only switch analytics on if you click "Accept all". Until then, it stays off and no information is sent to Google Analytics.

Advertising (not currently used)

We don't currently use advertising cookies. If that changes, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

5. Your choice: Accept all or Reject all

When you first visit our website, a cookie banner asks whether you're happy for us to use analytics.

- "Accept all" and "Reject all" are shown side by side, the same size and the same style, so saying no is as easy as saying yes.
- Until you make a choice, we only use what is strictly necessary. Analytics stays off.
- If you click "Reject all", you can still use the whole website and send us an enquiry.

We save your choice on your device so we don't ask you on every page. We'll ask you again if we change the cookies we use.

6. Changing your mind or withdrawing consent

You can change your choice, or withdraw your consent, at any time. It's as easy to withdraw as it was to give.

- On our website: click "Manage cookies" at the bottom of any page and change your choice. From then on, we stop using analytics. Cookies already on your device may stay there until they expire or you delete them in your browser settings.
- In your browser: most browsers let you see, block and delete cookies in their settings. If you block all cookies, some parts of our website may not work. The ICO explains more at ico.org.uk.

Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything that happened before you changed your mind.
