Cookie policy

This is a draft for review. It is linked from the footer so it can be read. It is not a final published policy.

Use Manage cookies in the footer to open the banner again. Accept and Reject are shown with equal weight, and Settings lets you choose Analytics and Marketing separately. Necessary storage stays on. Your choice is stored with localStorage inside a try/catch, so a blocked browser does not break the page.

Necessary

  • tnt-consent in localStorage. Remembers Accept, Reject or your saved settings.
  • tnt-utm in sessionStorage. Remembers a campaign reference so the WhatsApp message can include it. It is not sent to an ad network by itself.
  • tnt-packing in localStorage, on the holiday checklist page only. Remembers which packing items you ticked. The key includes the destination and departure date from the link, not your name.

Analytics, only after consent

  • Google Analytics 4, measurement ID held in the site configuration. Typical cookies include _ga and _ga_ followed by the measurement ID. The tag is not on the page until you allow Analytics.

Marketing, only after consent

  • Meta Pixel, only if a real pixel ID has been added. It may set cookies such as _fbp. {{PLACEHOLDER: confirm Meta Pixel ID}}
  • TikTok Pixel, only if a real pixel ID has been added. {{PLACEHOLDER: confirm TikTok Pixel ID}}

Other requests that are not cookies we set

  • Fonts are files on this website (Fraunces and Source Sans 3). The pages do not call fonts.googleapis.com when they load.
  • Aladhan at api.aladhan.com, when you ask for prayer times. Your browser sends the city. We do not set a cookie for that call. Mentioned here so the draft matches the feature.
  • Open-Meteo, when the holiday page loads a forecast. Your browser sends the destination from the link. We do not set a cookie for that call.
